# Goal

Status: active

## Mission

Finish the complete RAWG corpus as an exhaustive, provenance-backed master inventory of thousands of genuinely novel reusable kits that are authored, runtime-proven, admitted, and correctly organized into validated domains.

## Requirements

- Resume the append-only production workspace; never restart, overwrite evidence, sample away rare mechanics, or count rows, clusters, candidates, and build requests as built kits.
- Preserve all 881,069 source identities plus direct effects, interactions, timing, conditions, platform constraints, evidence-limited outcomes, pointer queries, and processing epochs.
- Use `gpt-5.6-luna` with low reasoning for Codex review and authoring, bounded to 16 concurrent lanes; keep deterministic and local-model evidence stages subordinate to source provenance.
- Process bounded waves through inventory comparison, fail-forward accept/reject/repair, capability-specific ESM authoring, contract tests, NexusSimulator runtime proof, atomic `kits/` admission, domain placement, and whole-inventory architecture audit.
- After every wave, reconcile every output, retry repairable failures, quarantine visible failures, record throughput and quality, remove only workflow-owned transient scratch, and start the next wave from durable checkpoints.
- Upgrade prompts, batch size, or concurrency only when measured evidence shows a faster configuration that preserves or improves valid yield.
- Hold below 10 GiB free and require approval before deleting historical evidence, writing outside KitUniverse, using credentials, publishing, deploying, or integrating with ProtoKits/Nexus Engine.
- Continue until the complete corpus and provenance reconcile and the inventory contains thousands of runtime-proven novel kits when supported; the current `$kit-universe-it` campaign is not successful until its fresh baseline gains at least 100 runtime-proven kits and 100 validated top-level domains.

## Current Checkpoint

- Production has 881,069 evidence maps and pointer maps, 123,392 completed extraction pages, 522,154 grounded observations, 31,301 direct master families, 6,430 pointer master families, and 18,845 completed local refinements.
- Of the refined masters, 6,301 require authoritative Codex review; production currently has zero Codex decisions, build requests, authored kits, runtime-proven kits, packages, domains, or memberships.
- Free disk is about 14 GiB, so the 10 GiB safety hold leaves little production headroom.

## Final Output

A durable reconciliation report proving exact corpus/provenance integrity, fresh-baseline kit and domain deltas, accepted IDs, authored packages, contract tests, runtime proofs, memberships, architecture results, epochs, failures, disk use, measured throughput, and the commands used to validate them.
